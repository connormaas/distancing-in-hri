# Distancing in Human-Robot Interaction

A replication of Mumm and Mutlu's *Human-Robot Proxemics: Physical and Psychological Distancing in Human-Robot Interaction*. Paper: [`distancing-in-hri.pdf`](distancing-in-hri.pdf)

## Abstract

Robots that respect personal space fit into daily life without getting in the way. We replicate the original study with a different robot (Misty instead of Wakamaru), varying its likability and gaze to measure how close people stand to it, and add a factor the original didn't test: participant personality.

## Implementation

The study ran on a [Misty](https://www.mistyrobotics.com) robot, programmed in [Blockly](https://developers.google.com/blockly) so every session ran automatically. The code, custom audio, and Blockly configurations are in `implementation/`.
